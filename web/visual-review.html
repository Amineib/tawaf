Landmarks and people, seen closer

Shared landmark models now have draped cloth, a separate door and curtain, a sloping marble base, a recessed Black Stone surround and an open Maqam enclosure. Eight new pilgrim silhouettes add shaped shoulders, hands, footwear and varied clothing. These fixed views were rendered on a rented NVIDIA H100.
